What value range can n-bit unsigned and signed integers represent? How is the overflow boundary determined?

Unsigned n-bit range is 0 to 2^n - 1. Signed two’s-complement n-bit range is -2^(n-1) to 2^(n-1)-1. Overflow boundary is crossed when arithmetic result lies outside these bounds.
