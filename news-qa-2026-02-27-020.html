State the principle of temporal locality. Under which conditions does data reuse improve performance?

Published: 27.02.2026
By Jan Kowalski

In News.

Temporal locality means recently used data is likely used again soon. Performance improves when reuse happens before eviction from cache. Tight loops and small working sets typically benefit most.
