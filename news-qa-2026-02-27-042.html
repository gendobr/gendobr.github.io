What is an addressing mode? Provide effective address (EA) formulas for register-indirect and base-index-displacement addressing.

Addressing mode defines how instruction operands are located. For register-indirect mode: EA = [Rbase]. For base-index-displacement mode: EA = Rbase + Rindex * scale + displacement (or EA = Base + Index + Disp in simpler ISAs).
